## Data Science & AI Career

I am Head of Data & AI at [The Sports Consultancy](https://thesportsconsultancy.com/), which specialises in the commercial side of sport, with over a decade of expertise in Data Science, Machine Learning and AI.
I lead our data and AI strategy, building our data foundation, pipelines, automations and agentic solutions that make the business more efficient.

I am also co-founder of [GN Basketball Intelligence](https://www.gn-basketball-intelligence.com/) (GN BI), a European basketball analytics company that helps scouts, general managers and coaches make sharper roster decisions with validated, transparent numbers. Our work covers:

- *Recruitment and roster building*: shortlists matched to a team's roster gaps, from top leagues to less-scouted markets.
- *Cross-league translation*: placing players from different leagues on a single scale, with projections for stepping up a level shown with their uncertainty, and validated against players who actually changed leagues.
- *Player archetypes*: role "fingerprints" and similarity scores that separate how a player plays from their raw production.
- *EuroLeague workbench*: lineup, player value, clutch, rotation and possession-quality analytics, including a live win-probability and momentum tracker.

We also built the [Euroleague Advanced API](https://euroleague-advanced-api.eu/), a REST API for advanced statistics on the EuroLeague and EuroCup. Basic stats are free to access, and a subscription unlocks the advanced endpoints, such as clutch stats by game, round or season, and gives faster access to the free data.

We work with clubs of all sizes, including Doxa Lefkadas, where we support team building, player recruitment, and performance and opponent scouting.

Previously, as Senior Director of Data Science at [WPP](https://www.wpp.com/), I leveraged *Agent Based Models (ABM)* to unravel the complexities of consumer behaviour.
I also drove several *Generative AI* MVPs, PoCs and community initiatives, focused on agentic solutions that reshape how we interact with our own data sources.
Additionally, I have been involved in *geospatial data science*, where I explored the intricate patterns of geographical data to derive meaningful insights. I presented my work in this area at the [Spatial Data Science Conference 2023](https://youtu.be/zKL6cuiOtcY?si=D-MClSFb4x4LEhqE).

With a rich background in both line and team management, I excel in stakeholder management, crafting data strategies and solutions, and nurturing teams towards achieving strategic goals. My consultancy expertise in data science projects has been instrumental in shaping innovative solutions across various domains.

I am a member and scientific collaborator at the [Sports Analytics Group](https://aueb-analytics.wixsite.com/sports), Department of Statistics at the Athens University of Economics and Business (AUEB), where I contribute with my work on basketball analytics. I am the author of the popular [Euroleague API](https://pypi.org/project/euroleague-api/) Python package. I have presented my work on basketball analytics at various conferences and workshops; this is my presentation at the [Data Science Festival 2025 in London](https://youtu.be/-v004dFa6HE?si=Ln2voOoHxnn6l-Xy).
